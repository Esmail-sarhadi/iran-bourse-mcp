# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming to everyone who cares about making Iranian market data usable by AI agents, whatever their experience, language or background. Persian and English are both first-class here.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be precise about data. Back claims about the market, the exchange or an endpoint with a reproducible example.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently, in Persian or English.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Posting other people's private information.
- Spreading investment tips, pump-and-dump content or promotions in issues, pull requests or discussions. This is a tooling project, not a signals channel.
- Requests to add order placement, credential handling or ways to bypass the exchange's access controls.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Report a problem through GitHub's private reporting on this repository or by contacting the maintainer listed in the repository profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
